# Decide

Use this skill when the [Decisions section](/docs/conventions/workflow.md#decisions) of the workflow says to stop.

1. Stop the task and say what stopped it: the decision the task needs, an option that would change an agreed point of the task or a rule of work, with the reasons for the points or rules it would change, or that a reason is unavailable, and why you recommend it, or the rules that contradict each other or make the task impossible.
2. Find the [decision records](/docs/conventions/decisions.md) about this decision, where the decision record rules say they live, and the entries in [open questions](/docs/open-questions.md) about it. The Decisions section says when an option that a record rejected can be proposed.
3. Collect the facts that weigh on the choice from the code, the [documents](/docs/glossary.md#document) and the documentation of the tools involved.
4. List every option worth considering with its advantages and drawbacks, as the Options section of a decision record lists them. Say which option you recommend and why.
5. Ask the user to decide, and wait for the answer.
6. After the decision, follow the workflow: the [Starting section](/docs/conventions/workflow.md#starting) on a decided open question and the Decisions section on decision records. Then continue the task.
