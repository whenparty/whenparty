# Decide

Use this skill when the [Decisions section](/docs/conventions/workflow-changes.md#decisions) of the workflow says to stop.

1. Stop the task and say what stopped it: the decision the task needs, or the rules that contradict each other or make the task impossible.
2. Find the [decision records](/docs/conventions/decisions.md) about this decision, where the decision record rules say they live, and the entries in [open questions](/docs/open-questions.md) about it. The Decisions section says when an option that a record has already weighed can be proposed again.
3. Collect the facts that weigh on the choice from the code, the documents and the documentation of the tools involved.
4. List every option worth considering with its advantages and drawbacks, as the Options section of a decision record lists them. Say which option you recommend and why.
5. Ask the user to decide, and wait for the answer.
6. After the decision, follow the workflow: the Starting section on a decided open question and the Decisions section on decision records. Then continue the task.
