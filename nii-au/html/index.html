01 / THE IDEA

Good things begin
with a small mark.

We think creativity should feel less like a performance, and more like opening a window.

  1. 01 ✳

    Take a spark.

    One thoughtfully small invitation to notice, draw, fold, or imagine.

  2. 02 ✳

    Make a mark.

    Grab a pen, a scrap of paper, or whatever happens to be nearby.

  3. 03 ✳

    See what grows.

    Keep your little creation. The joy is in making, not getting it right.

No pressure. No masterpieces required.
