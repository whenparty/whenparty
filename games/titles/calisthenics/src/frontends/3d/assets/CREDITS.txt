Calisthenics character and animation assets

Source: Mesh2Motion / Quaternius, CC0.
https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app
https://mesh2motion.org/#features
https://quaternius.com/

The athlete.glb model is repacked from static/animations/human-addon-animations.glb.
Included clips: Idle_Subtle, Pushup, Kneeling Tired, Victory Fist Pump,
Confused, Tired Hunched and Death_B. Unused animation buffers and texture references are removed.

The application supplies its own face, hair, clothing colors and muscle morphs.
