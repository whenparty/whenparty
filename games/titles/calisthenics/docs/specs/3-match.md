# Match

- `3-1` The room has 12 seats. In the lobby each seated player enters an athlete name of 1 to 20 characters after trimming spaces. Names may repeat.
- `3-2` The host can start the championship when 2 to 12 players are seated and every one of them has entered a name. Each of them, the host included, gets an athlete. No athletes are added later. A player without an athlete sees the championship but cannot act. Starting the championship opens the first challenge's announcement.
- `3-3` Each challenge has three screens:
  1. Announcement. Everyone sees the challenge's number and name, but not its requirements or predicted scores, so the players can talk about it. The host can open preparation.
  2. Preparation. Each athlete's player makes and confirms a plan ([1-2](1-preparation.md)). Once every athlete's plan is confirmed, the host can start the challenge.
  3. Results. The athletes perform in the shared queue (3-5). After an athlete's turn ends, everyone sees the levels this challenge's plan trained, its score, whether that is full or partial completion or not performed ([2-2](2-scoring.md)), and the reason for a score below 100. Result cards of athletes whose turns have not ended stay hidden; their performance and reaction may already show how the attempt went. After every turn has ended, everyone sees the championship standings and the host can open the next challenge's announcement. After the tenth challenge's queue ends, the results also show the winners, there is no next announcement, and the championship ends. No new championship starts in that room.
- `3-4` While an athlete's player is absent or has left the room, the host cannot move the championship to its next screen or advance the performance queue. The current turn's timer pauses and keeps its remaining time until every athlete's player is back. An athlete whose player has left keeps no player, so the championship stays paused. The game itself never ends a championship before the tenth challenge. When a player counts as absent or as having left, and closing the room when the host leaves, follow the [platform's session rules](../../../../platform/p2p/STATES.md).
- `3-5` Results begin with the first athlete's turn, and athletes take turns in seat order. Each turn lasts twelve seconds in total, including the attempt and reaction, then the next athlete's turn starts automatically. The host can end the current turn early with Next athlete; other players cannot advance it. An ended turn cannot be ended again to skip another athlete. The last turn's end completes the queue. Everyone, including players of the text interface and spectators, sees the same current athlete. Reloading or switching interfaces continues the current turn and its remaining time instead of restarting the queue. The host cannot open the next challenge before the queue ends.
