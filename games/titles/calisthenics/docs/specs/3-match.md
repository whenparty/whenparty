# Match

- `3-1` The room has 12 seats. In the lobby each seated player enters an athlete name of 1 to 20 characters after trimming spaces and, in the 3D interface, may choose an appearance ([4-1](4-presentation.md)). Names may repeat. When a player leaves their seat in the lobby ([4-6](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)), their name and appearance ([4-1](4-presentation.md)) leave with them, so a player who takes that seat later starts without them. When a player's leave fails in the game ([5-10](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/5-matches.md)), every seated player loses their name and appearance.
- `3-2` The host ([2-3](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/2-page.md)) can start the championship when no match has started in the room ([4-2](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)), 2 to 12 players are seated and every one of them has entered a name. A recovery of the host's page from a record whose match number it cannot read counts as no match started ([10-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/10-recovery.md)). Each seated player, the host included, gets an athlete. No athletes are added later. A player without an athlete sees the championship but cannot act. Starting the championship opens the first challenge's announcement.
- `3-3` Each challenge has three screens:
  1. Announcement. Everyone sees the challenge's number and name, but not its requirements or predicted scores, so the players can talk about it. The host can open preparation.
  2. Preparation. Each athlete's player makes and confirms a plan ([1-2](1-preparation.md)). Once every athlete's plan is confirmed, the host can start the challenge.
  3. Results. The athletes perform in the shared queue (3-5). After an athlete's turn ends, everyone sees the levels this challenge's plan trained, its score, whether that is full or partial completion or not performed ([2-2](2-scoring.md)), and the reason for a score below 100. Result cards of athletes whose turns have not ended stay hidden; their performance and reaction may already show how the attempt went. After every turn has ended, everyone sees the championship standings and the host can open the next challenge's announcement. After the tenth challenge's queue ends, the results also show the winners, there is no next announcement, and the championship ends. No new championship starts in that room, except as 3-2 says.
- `3-4` An athlete's player is absent while it is lost or its page is hidden ([4-8](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)), and has left the room once it leaves its seat ([4-6](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)). While its connection stays open, a hidden page never makes its player leave. While an athlete's player is absent or has left the room, the host cannot move the championship to its next screen or advance the performance queue. The current turn's timer pauses and keeps its remaining time while the championship is paused, which is while an athlete's player is absent or has left the room; it runs again only when the championship goes from paused to not paused, so a player who is back again, or another player who comes back while one is still absent, changes no timer. An athlete whose player has left keeps no player, so the championship stays paused. The game itself never ends a championship before the tenth challenge.
- `3-5` Results begin with the first athlete's turn, and athletes take turns in seat order. Each turn lasts twelve seconds in total, including the attempt and reaction, then the next athlete's turn starts automatically, at the room time ([6-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/6-time.md)) at which the host's page ends the previous one, so every athlete has twelve seconds. The host can end the current turn early with Next athlete; other players cannot advance it. An ended turn cannot be ended again to skip another athlete. The last turn's end completes the queue. Everyone, including players of the text interface and spectators, sees the same current athlete. Reloading or switching interfaces continues the current turn and its remaining time instead of restarting the queue. The host cannot open the next challenge before the queue ends.
- `3-6` The game plays through the netcode ([1-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/1-rules.md)) with the app ID `when.party/calisthenics`, a waiting time of 600 seconds, which the rooms module uses for a lost player ([6-7](../../../../platform/rooms/docs/specs/6-presence.md)), a lost host ([3-7](../../../../platform/rooms/docs/specs/3-participant.md)) and an idle room ([4-7](../../../../platform/rooms/docs/specs/4-owner.md)), and format version 1. Each change to the form of its states or actions comes with a higher format version. It gives the netcode no "admit", so before the championship anyone with the room code enters while a seat is free ([4-2](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)).
- `3-7` When a match has started in the room ([4-2](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/4-seats.md)) and the host's state is the lobby again, because the host's page recovered the room without its championship ([10-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/10-recovery.md)) or a player's leave failed in the game ([5-10](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/5-matches.md)), seated players enter names and choose appearances as 3-1 says, the host cannot start a championship (3-2), and the status says that the room lost its championship, as [7-1](7-status.md) gives it.
