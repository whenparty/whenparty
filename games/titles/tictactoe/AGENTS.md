# Tic-tac-toe

`tictactoe/` is the tic-tac-toe game of [titles](../AGENTS.md), for two players and anyone who watches. It plays through the [netcode module](../../platform/netcode/AGENTS.md) of the platform and is one [deployment](/docs/glossary.md#deployment).

- [docs/specs/](docs/specs/): its specification of its rules, its page and what the page shows.
- [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md): which files of `src/` hold the rules, the page, the texts of the status and the binding to the document.
