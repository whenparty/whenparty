# Tic-tac-toe

`tictactoe/` is the tic-tac-toe game of [titles](../AGENTS.md), for two players and anyone who watches. It plays through the [netcode module](../../platform/netcode/AGENTS.md) of the platform. [src/tictactoe.ts](src/tictactoe.ts) holds its rules and `src/main.ts` its page; the deployment uses `compose.yml` and `Dockerfile`.

- [docs/specs/](docs/specs/): its specification of its rules, its page and what the page shows.
