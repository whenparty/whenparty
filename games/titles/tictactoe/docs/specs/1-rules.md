# Rules

- `1-1` The game plays through the netcode ([1-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/1-rules.md)) with the app ID `when.party/tictactoe`, a waiting time of 600 seconds, format version 1 and two seats, X and O, in that order. Each change to the form of its states or moves comes with a higher format version. It gives the netcode no "admit" and no "can start", so a match can start only when both seats have players ([5-2](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/5-matches.md)). Each match starts with an empty board of three rows of three cells, with X to move.
- `1-2` While a match is not over and not paused, when the player on the seat to move marks an empty cell, the cell gets the seat's mark, and the other seat is to move.
- `1-3` A move names a cell and the marks of every cell of the board it was made for. It is refused ([8-5](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/8-actions.md)) when those marks differ from the board of the match, such as when it is sent again ([8-7](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/8-actions.md)) after a recovery of the host ([10-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/10-recovery.md)) lost an earlier move, and so is every move that 1-2 does not allow.
- `1-4` When the three cells of a row, a column or a diagonal hold the same mark, that mark wins and the match is over. The cells of every such line are the winning cells.
- `1-5` When the board is full and no mark has won, the match is a draw and is over.
- `1-6` While at least one player of the match is lost or its page is hidden ([1-2](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/1-rules.md)), the match is paused. When no player is lost or hidden any more, the match goes on with the same board and the same seat to move.
- `1-7` When a player's page has been hidden for 600 seconds of room time ([6-1](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/6-time.md)) in a row during the match, the match is over without a winner, and its result names that player's seat; when two players reach 600 seconds at the same room time, it names the first of their seats in the order of the seats. The count of a player starts when the game is told "hidden" about it and its count does not run, and stops when the player is visible again; a "hidden" while its count runs, such as the repeats of [1-5](../../../../platform/netcode/docs/specs/1-rules.md), does not restart it.
- `1-8` When a player leaves while the match is not over, the match is over without a winner, and its result names the seat that left.
- `1-9` Once a match is over, presence events and deadlines leave its state as it is.
