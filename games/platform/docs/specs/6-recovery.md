# Host recovery

- `6-1` When a page of the host gains control after the game started ([5-3](../../rooms/docs/specs/5-pages.md)), the host recovers on that page. Recovery starts when the page gets the term and the roster ([5-5](../../rooms/docs/specs/5-pages.md)), and ends when every participant that the roster names "online" has sent its last snapshot, or 10 seconds after it started, whichever comes first.
- `6-2` While the host recovers, the netcode tells the host's page and, once their peer connections to the host open, the participants' pages that the host recovers, and each participant's page sends the host its last snapshot, or none. The host drops every action, puts off every other change until recovery ends, and its timers and room time stop.
- `6-3` When recovery ends, the host takes the newest snapshot ([3-5](3-snapshots.md)) of those it got and the one in its page's record ([5-1](../../rooms/docs/specs/5-pages.md)). It removes from the seats and the queue every participant that the roster names "left", and makes the change that ends recovery with the result, in its new term. When it has no snapshot, the result has no match, and the participants that have not left take the seats in the order of their public numbers, and the queue after them. Then it sets its timers from the state, and room time goes on from that of the result.
- `6-4` A page of the host that keeps control, such as one whose scripts the browser suspended and resumed, keeps its term and its game, and does not recover.
- `6-5` After recovery, a change that only snapshots the host did not get held is lost. A participant's page takes the snapshot of the new term ([3-5](3-snapshots.md)) and sends an action that awaits its result again ([4-6](4-actions.md)).
