# The p2p relay

The relay in [src/server.mjs](../src/server.mjs) serves the [p2p module](../../p2p/AGENTS.md). Its limits and heartbeat follow from what a tab sends and from what `@trystero-p2p/ws-relay` does not do. They apply only to the relay's clients; the transport server has its own ([7-server.md](../../transport/docs/specs/7-server.md)).

- A tab of a game that plays through p2p holds one WebSocket with 2 subscriptions, its room and its own peer topic. It sends messages under 1 KB: about 5 for each other player in the room while it connects to them, then one a minute. The limits leave a wide margin over that and keep one client from taking the relay's capacity: 5,000 connections, 40,000 subscriptions, 8 per socket, 65,536 bytes per message, over which a socket closes with 1009, and a burst of 200 messages, enough to join a room of 40, refilled at 20 a second. With the 64 connections per address that [games.conf](/infra/nginx/conf.d/games.conf) allows ([2-4](/infra/docs/specs/2-headers.md)), it takes 79 addresses to fill the relay, and subscriptions never run out before connections do.
- The relay closes a socket over its burst or rate with 1008, and the client reconnects after a few seconds. The rate limit hears each message before `@trystero-p2p/ws-relay` does, so the relay does not pass on the message that closes the socket: it ignores messages from a socket that is not open.
- `@trystero-p2p/ws-relay` never pings. The relay pings each of its clients every 30 seconds and terminates the ones that did not answer the previous ping, so idle sockets stay open through nginx ([2-5](/infra/docs/specs/2-headers.md)) and Cloudflare, and dead ones, such as those of a phone that lost its network, release their subscriptions.
