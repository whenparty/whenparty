# Leaving and the end of a room

- `8-1` When the app of a participant's page leaves, the page tells the rooms server, when its signaling connection is open, and the owner's page, when its peer connection is open ([3-14](../../../transport/docs/specs/3-connection.md)), that the participant leaves. It then deletes the record, closes its connections ([4-5](../../../transport/docs/specs/4-delivery.md)) and lets the lock go. The server and the owner's page remove the participant when the leave reaches them ([11-7](11-server-participants.md), [6-7](6-presence.md)).
- `8-2` When the app of the owner's page leaves, the page tells the rooms server, when its signaling connection is open, that the owner leaves, and sends "room closed" to each participant whose peer connection is open for rooms. It then deletes the record, closes its connections ([4-5](../../../transport/docs/specs/4-delivery.md)) and lets the lock go. The room closes when the server learns of the leave, or else as [10-3](10-server-room.md) says.
- `8-3` When the app leaves while creating, joining or returning has not completed, the page ends it: a page that asked to join and was not let in withdraws its join request when its signaling connection is open, a page that has a token leaves as 8-1 and 8-2 say, and every page closes its connections and lets the lock go.
- `8-4` When creating, joining or returning fails, or the room ends for a page, the app is told once, with the reason. When the room ends, the page then deletes the record, closes its connections and lets the lock go. When creating, joining or returning fails, the page closes its connections and lets the lock go, and the record stays as it is.
- `8-5` After the app leaves, or creating, joining or returning fails, or the room ends, the page tells the app nothing more and ignores every later answer or event of what it did before: such an answer does not write the record, open a connection or reach the app. Leaving then changes nothing.
- `8-6` When the transport refuses a signaling connection as outdated ([1-2](../../../transport/docs/specs/1-signaling.md)), creating, or joining that has not completed, fails with "outdated", and otherwise the room ends for the page with "outdated". The app is also told which side is older, the page or the server, as the transport says. The owner's page then sends "outdated" with that side to each participant whose peer connection is open for rooms, and a participant's page that gets it tells its app that the room ended with "outdated" and that side, the owner's page or the server.
