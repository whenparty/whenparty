# Texts, paths and closing messages

- `4-1` A text is a string of at most 65,536 bytes of UTF-8 with no lone surrogates. When the [module](/docs/glossary.md#module) sends another value, or sends while its side is not open ([3-1](3-connection.md)), sending fails and nothing is sent.
- `4-2` A text that a side sends after it became open arrives at the other side once and in the order sent, unless the peer connection closed before it arrived. The sender's ready ([3-6](3-connection.md)) arrives before it, so the other side is open by then or becomes open on that ready. A side that is not yet open drops a text that arrives. A side drops a message that is neither a text nor one of the transport's own (4-3), and a text over the limit of 4-1. When the module has no handler of texts, or the handler throws, a text that arrives counts as delivered, and delivery goes on.
- `4-3` The transport's own checks, ready and closing messages travel on the channel in one queue with the texts, and do not count toward the 65,536 bytes of a text.
- `4-4` When a side closes other than because a closing message arrived, it sends the other side a closing message with its reason, or with `closed` when it closed without one, as in [3-3](3-connection.md) and [3-12](3-connection.md). Before the side is open, it sends the message through the transport server while its own signaling connection is open, and also on the channel when its channel is open; once open, only on the channel.
- `4-5` A closing side closes its channel, which lets the browser deliver what was already sent on it, and then closes the browser's connection when the channel has closed or 1 second has passed, or at once when its channel is not open. A closing message may therefore not arrive: on the channel it waits behind the texts already sent.
- `4-6` The path of a peer connection is direct or through the TURN server, and its round-trip time is in milliseconds, or unknown while the browser has not measured it. Whenever an open side notices that the chosen pair of candidates changed, such as between direct and through the TURN server or to another network, the transport tells the module the new path and round-trip time. While the page's scripts run, the side notices a change within 5 seconds. When the module's handler of this notice throws, the transport goes on.
- `4-7` When a closing message of the other side arrives at a side that is not closed, the side closes with `closed`, or with `too small` when the message carries that reason.
- `4-8` While a side is open, it sends a check every 5 seconds from the moment it opened, and the other side answers each check at once, also while the browser slows its timers. When nothing has arrived at an open side for 15 seconds, its browser reports that the connection failed or closed, its channel closes without a closing message, or its browser fails to send, the side closes with `broken`. A report that the connection is disconnected closes no side, and checks alone keep a side open.
- `4-9` A side drops a message of the transport for which no requirement gives an outcome in its state, such as a second ready, or a check that arrives before the other side's ready.
