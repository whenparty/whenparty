# blog

`blog/` is the [project](/docs/glossary.md#project) of the When Party blog at `blog.when.party`: Markdown posts that Eleventy builds into static pages, which an nginx container serves behind the nginx proxy in [`infra/`](/infra/AGENTS.md). It is one [deployment](/docs/glossary.md#deployment).

- [docs/posts.md](docs/posts.md): how to write a post: its folder and address, its front matter, images and the `#side` mark, the picture of its link preview and how to refresh link previews after a deploy, formulas, drafts and audio. Read it before adding or changing a post or `src/posts/posts.11tydata.js`.
- [docs/site.md](docs/site.md): what `eleventy.config.js` adds to Markdown, how styles, images and audio get URLs that change with their content, the cache headers of `nginx.conf`, how the container mounts the audio folder and the limits it runs with, which page the look follows, the window width at which side cards move into the margin and the widths it depends on, the Content-Security-Policy, how to run the site locally and how it deploys. Read it before changing `eleventy.config.js`, `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `.dockerignore`, `src/_includes/`, `src/index.njk`, `src/styles.css`, `nginx.conf`, the `Dockerfile` or `docker-compose.yml`.
- [docs/podcast.md](docs/podcast.md): the podcast feed `/podcast.xml`, which of Apple's rules it follows and how, its cover, why players in a browser on another site cannot play the audio, the audio traffic it adds, how a post becomes an episode, how the show is submitted to Apple Podcasts and how the feed would move. Read it before changing `src/podcast.njk`, `src/podcast-cover.jpg`, the `audio` field of a post or the `podcastEpisodes` filter in `eleventy.config.js`.
- [docs/audio.md](docs/audio.md): the audio folder on the VPS, the SFTP-only user `blogaudio` and how to set it up, and how to upload audio. Read it before uploading audio, setting up a VPS for the blog or changing the mount of `audio/` in `docker-compose.yml`.
- [docs/decisions/separate-host.md](docs/decisions/separate-host.md): why the blog is a project of its own on `blog.when.party` and not pages of `www/`.
- [docs/decisions/site-generator.md](docs/decisions/site-generator.md): why Eleventy builds the pages.
- [docs/decisions/formulas.md](docs/decisions/formulas.md): why KaTeX turns formulas into MathML at build time.
- [docs/decisions/share-image.md](docs/decisions/share-image.md): why the link preview of a post shows a JPEG cut from the cover and not the WebP cover. Read it before changing the link preview tags in `src/_includes/base.njk` or the `shareImage` field.
- [docs/decisions/side-cards.md](docs/decisions/side-cards.md): why a side card stands in the margin and stays in view beside its block.
- [docs/decisions/audio-storage.md](docs/decisions/audio-storage.md): why the audio files live in a folder on the VPS, outside git and the image.
- [docs/decisions/audio-upload.md](docs/decisions/audio-upload.md): why an SFTP-only user without a chroot uploads them.
- [docs/decisions/podcast-ai-disclosure.md](docs/decisions/podcast-ai-disclosure.md): why the podcast says that its audio is generated with AI in its descriptions and not in the audio. Read it before changing the descriptions in `src/podcast.njk`.
- [/infra/docs/sites.md](/infra/docs/sites.md): how the nginx proxy reaches the site by its container name and the `wp_www` network. Read it before renaming the service, the container or the network in `docker-compose.yml`.
