# Working on the site

- The `www.when.party` row of `2-2` in [2-headers.md](/infra/docs/specs/2-headers.md) states the page's Content-Security-Policy, and the nginx proxy sets it in [www.conf](/infra/nginx/conf.d/www.conf), which ships with the [nginx deploy](/.github/docs/deploys.md#infrayml), not with this folder. The policy blocks inline styles and scripts, so styles stay in `html/styles.css` and scripts in `.js` files in `html/`, such as `takeoff.js`. A new outside resource, such as another font host, needs a change to `www.conf`.
- To meet [serving.md](/docs/conventions/serving.md), `index.html` loads `styles.css` and each script with `?v=` and the first 8 hex digits that `shasum` prints for it. A change to one of them updates its `?v=` in the same commit.
- Wide screens are 800px and wider. Every balloon's SVG has the `viewBox` 0 0 200 210 and is drawn 200 by 210px, so its coordinates are px from the top left of `.balloon-float`. It has three strings from 84 140, under its knot. On wide screens the balloons are tied one under another along the vertical rope, odd ones on its left and even ones on its right, by `string-side`, which `:nth-child(even)` mirrors. Below 800px they stand in a row over the horizontal rope, odd ones on `string-short` and even ones on `string-long`. These two slant by 5deg, the `--lean` below 800px, so that at rest each knot is straight above the point where its string meets the rope. A change to that `--lean` changes them too.
- `.balloon-float` is placed so that the end of the string it shows at rest, `--anchor-x` and `--anchor-y`, lies on the rope. The balloon tilts, bobs and scales around this point, and a change to the end of a string changes it too. On wide screens the point is the top left corner of the balloon's `li`: the padding of the `ul` puts the first one at `--rope-x` and `--first-anchor`, and each `li` is 105px high. The vertical rope starts 48px above the first anchor and ends 31px above the bottom of `.places`, inside the bow of the weight. Below 800px the point is in the middle of the `li`, at `--rope-y`.
- The dashed balloon scales its `.balloon-body`, which holds its outline and knot, and its name to .68 around 84 140, so its strings and anchors are those of the others. `.balloon-name` gives that point as `52px 130px`, from its own top left corner at 32 10.
- A new balloon copies the `li` of the games balloon, before the dashed one, and gets a class, such as `.balloon-blog`, that sets `--wash` and `--strong` to two colours defined in both `:root` blocks of `styles.css`, and its `--inflate-delay` and `--bob`. The delays rise by .1s in list order, so the balloons inflate one after another, and no two share a `--bob`, so they do not bob in step. Its position in the list decides its side, its tilt and its string.
- The boxes of the balloons overlap, so only the fill and the knot of a balloon take clicks and hover.
- Browsers cannot animate a navigation to another origin, so on a click `takeoff.js` adds `is-flying` to the balloon and `is-leaving` to `body`, and opens the link after `takeoffMs`, a little before `takeoff` ends. In `styles.css` these classes start the `takeoff` animation and the fade, and on wide screens swap `string-side` for the hanging string the balloon shows below 800px. Change the two durations together. `takeoff.js` also sets `inert` on the HTML siblings of the balloon and of each of its ancestors below `body`, so that neither the mouse nor the keyboard reaches another link during the takeoff. `is-flying` turns off the clicks and hover of the balloon's own fill and knot, which are not inert. The balloon's link stays focusable, so while `body` has `is-leaving`, `takeoff.js` cancels a click on it without a modifier key and starts nothing: Enter on the link then neither opens it at once nor sets a second timer that opens it again. `is-flying` also removes the `refilter` of `.balloon-art`, so that it restarts with the inflate after Back, as [balloon-sharpness.md](decisions/balloon-sharpness.md) explains. When Back restores the page from the back-forward cache, `takeoff.js` removes `is-flying`, `is-leaving` and every `inert`. `styles.css` has no takeoff under reduced motion, so `takeoff.js` opens the link at once there.
- With `non-scaling-stroke`, the `draw` dash of a rope is as many px long as its path is in `viewBox` units, so a rope longer on the page than in its `viewBox` ends short. Both `viewBox`es are 600 units long. The vertical rope is 17px plus 105px per balloon long, so it ends short from the sixth balloon, and the horizontal one is at most 508px.
- Through the proxy, every 404 of the site shows the shared 404 page, as `3-2` of [3-sites.md](/infra/docs/specs/3-sites.md) says, and `/_errors/` belongs to the proxy, as [nginx-configs.md](/infra/docs/nginx-configs.md#shared-settings-and-a-sites-own) says. A 404 page of the site's own needs a change to `www.conf`.
- The container sends none of the [proxy's headers](/infra/docs/specs/2-headers.md), such as the Content-Security-Policy, so a local run does not show what they block. To run the site locally:

  ```bash
  cd www
  docker build -t www:local .
  docker run --rm -p 3001:80 www:local
  ```

  Then open http://localhost:3001.
- As [deploys.md](/.github/docs/deploys.md) says, a push to `main` that changes files in `www/` other than Markdown runs [www.yml](/.github/workflows/www.yml), which deploys through [site.yml](/.github/workflows/site.yml). [deploys.md](/.github/docs/deploys.md) also says which workflow changes redeploy the site.
